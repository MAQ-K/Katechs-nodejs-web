## Cairo is loaded per-route now, not sitewide

`pages/_document.js` dropped Cairo when the site moved to Almarai-only, and its comment says nothing
references it any more. **That is no longer true** — every `components/HpNew/**` section asks for
Cairo by name, and has been silently falling back to `system-ui` since. The design specifies Cairo for
every heading.

Fix: the Cairo stylesheet link was added to **`pages/hp-new.js`'s `<Head>`**, not `_document.js`, so
the extra request lands on this one noindex route and the frozen `pages/index.js` gains nothing.
Weights `400;500;600;700;800` (500 is for `HeroNav.js`; Cairo, unlike Almarai, really does ship every
weight). `components/HpNew/README.md` rule 7 was corrected — it still claimed Cairo was in `_document.js`.

## Two bugs found and fixed along the way

- **`WebServicesPlans.js` rendered empty CTA buttons.** The design's mock data renames the plan CTA to
  `ctaText`/`ctaHref`; the real `data/services/data.js` carries `cta: { text, href }`. Caught in the
  first screenshot pass.
- **The store bento would not stack on a phone.** `.hp-store-box.is-build` / `.is-manage` are (0,2,0)
  and kept pinning themselves to grid columns 2 and 3 of a one-column grid — specificity beats source
  order, so the `max-width: 991px` block had to name them explicitly. Before the fix the bento was
  three implicit columns wide at 390px.

- **Carried over from SeoShowcase and fixed in the fork:** its rank meter ran `setInterval(…, 40)` from
  mount and never stopped — ~25 re-renders a second, on- and off-screen, with no reduced-motion guard.
  `Marketing.js` runs it only while the band is intersecting, and pins it at 100 under reduced motion.
  ⚠️ **`components/Common/SeoShowcase.js` still has this bug** and still runs on `pages/index.js`.
