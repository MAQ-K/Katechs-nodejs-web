[Katechs Website
Content Direction & Writing Rules
Purpose of This Document
This guide defines how website content must be written for Katechs. All content must follow these rules without exception.
Primary Website Goal
Help the visitor choose the right service in 5 seconds, then take action (contact Katechs).
Focus & Priority
Web Services are the main focus (≈80%)
 All services must appear:
 Equal in quality
 Equal in professionalism
 Equally trustworthy
 Focusing on web services does not mean other services are lower quality
The 5-Second Rule (Very Important)
 The visitor must understand and choose a service in 5 seconds or less
 Content must guide decisions, not explain details
 No technical thinking should be required
If a section needs more than 5 seconds to understand, it must be rewritten.
Web Services Content Principles
 Reduce confusion and decision anxiety
 Make the user feel safe choosing
 Reassure the user that Katechs handles all technical decisions
 Technology choice is not the user’s responsibility
Language & Writing Style
 Very clear, simple, human language
 Written for non-technical users
 Short sentences only
 No long paragraphs
 One idea per line
 Use “Best for…” and similar guiding phrases
Services Presentation Rules
 All services must have the same visual weight
 All services must sound professional and reliable
 Supporting services should feel connected to and supportive of web services
What to Avoid
 Long explanations
 Technical terms
 Complex sentences
 Marketing hype
 Feature-heavy descriptions
How Success Is Measured
Content is approved if:
 It is understood instantly
 It removes confusion
 The next step is obvious
 The visitor feels guided, not sold to
Content is rejected if:
 It explains instead of guiding
 It increases thinking or hesitation
Final Rule
If a section cannot be understood in 5 seconds, it does not belong on the website.]
